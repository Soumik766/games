What it is

Train the Ant is a virtual pet with a real little mind. The ant decides what to do for itself, and it learns from what happens next: a treat or a pet makes it more likely to do something again, a poke makes it less likely. Feed it, play with it, teach it to come to your finger, and watch its habits form. Only one ant lives in the box at a time.

  • It learns from you Every treat, pet and poke shapes how the ant behaves, so no two ants turn out the same.
  • Care for it Drop crumbs and sugar cubes, toss it a ball, or pick it up and carry it where you want it.
  • Life and memory A body stays where it falls, and its memories can be copied into a new ant that carries on what the old one learned.
  • A lineage Every ant that has lived in the box is remembered, with its name and how its story ended.

Progress

There is no account and no sign-in. Your ant, its memories and your settings are kept on your phone, and the ant carries on living while the app is closed.

Ads and purchases

Train the Ant is free and supported by ads: a small banner, an occasional full-screen ad when you summon a new ant or come back to the game, and short videos you can choose to watch for a golden sugar cube or to bring a dead ant back. There are no purchases. The game never puts the way forward behind an ad.

What is collected, and by whom, is set out in the privacy policy. The rules you agree to when you play are in the terms of use.
