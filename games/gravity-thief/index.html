What it is

Gravity Thief is a puzzle game about one idea: gravity always pulls toward the bottom of your screen, and you control which way that is. Every spin sends the thief falling, along with every crate, guard and balloon in the vault. A move that solves one problem usually creates another, and the exit stays shut until the last gem is gone.

  • Spin the whole vault Drag sideways or tap to turn the vault a quarter at a time, and watch everything fall.
  • Steal, then escape Getting to the gems and getting out are two different puzzles. Some vaults start an alarm the moment you finish the first.
  • Everything obeys gravity Drop guards onto spikes, stack crates against lasers and pressure plates, and mind the balloons, which fall the other way.
  • A perfect heist Every vault has a shortest route. Find it for three stars, and spend your coins on new disguises.

Progress

There is no account and no sign-in. Your progress, coins and disguises are kept on your phone, and the game plays offline.

Ads and purchases

Gravity Thief is free and supported by ads: a small banner, and short videos you can choose to watch to rewind a spin, earn hints or coins, double a reward, or skip a vault you are stuck on. There are no purchases. The game never puts the way forward behind an ad.

Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before showing ads, and you can change your choice in its Settings. What is collected, and by whom, is set out in the privacy policy. The rules you agree to when you play are in the terms of use.
