Snakebait: Run Away!

In every snake game, the snake eats the bait. Not this time. You are a wobbly little jelly in a garden arena, and hungry snakes are crawling out of the hedge to gobble you up. Drag anywhere to run, juke at the last second, and outsmart them.

What it is

Snakebait flips the classic snake game upside down: you are the one being chased. One bite and the run is over, so every escape counts. Sharp turns and fake-outs leave snakes snapping at thin air, stones and brick walls send them tumbling, and two snakes that collide knock each other dizzy. Level Mode is a set of short survival challenges with three stars to earn in each; Endless Mode keeps going until you are lunch.

  • Outsmart, don't outrun Lure snakes into stones, walls, each other, or their own tails.
  • Close calls charge your dash Skim past snapping jaws to fill the meter, then blast out of trouble.
  • Levels and endless Survive 5, 15 and 30 seconds for stars, or chase your best time and score.
  • Jelly flavours and arena themes Cute characters to collect with coins earned by playing, and garden themes to play in.

Progress and Google Play Games

You can play as a guest: progress is kept on your phone. If you sign in with Google Play Games, your progress is also saved in your own Google account, so it comes back when you reinstall or change phones. You can switch that backup off in the game's Settings at any time.

Ads and purchases

Snakebait is free and supported by ads: a small banner, an occasional full-screen ad between runs (never during one), and short videos you can choose to watch for a second chance or bonus coins. Optional purchases remove the banner and full-screen ads, add coins, or unlock arena themes. Everything can also be earned by playing. The game never puts the way forward behind an ad or a purchase.

What is collected, and by whom, is set out in the privacy policy. The rules you agree to when you play are in the terms of use.
