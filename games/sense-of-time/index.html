What it is

Sense of Time is an arcade game about timing. Some challenges test how quickly you react; others test how accurately you can feel time passing, with no clock to watch. The campaign runs through five worlds, from the basics to fake signals, invisible timers and boss levels that combine everything, with three stars to earn on every level. Every result is measured in milliseconds, so you can see yourself improve.

  • Stop the clock Hit an exact time with the timer showing, fading out, or never shown at all.
  • Hit the window Tap while the orb is inside the zone, as zones shrink, drift, multiply and hide traps.
  • Ignore the fakes React only to the real signal while wrong colours, wrong shapes and empty rounds try to catch you out.
  • Daily, duels and endless One daily challenge that is the same for everyone, head-to-head codes to share with friends, and an endless mode after the campaign.

Progress

There is no account and no sign-in. Your progress, records and settings are kept on your phone. Head-to-head duels work through a short code you share yourself; nothing is sent to us.

Ads and purchases

Sense of Time is free and supported by ads: a small banner, an occasional full-screen ad between levels (never during one), and short videos you can choose to watch to double the points from a run or to continue an endless run. There are no purchases. The game never puts the way forward behind an ad.

What is collected, and by whom, is set out in the privacy policy. The rules you agree to when you play are in the terms of use.
