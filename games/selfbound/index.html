What it is

Selfbound is a single path of moral dilemmas, read rather than played. Nothing is timed except the questions where hesitating is itself the answer, and nothing can be undone. The game keeps a quiet record of every choice and, at the end, shows you the shape of it.

  • Questions with no correct answer Every option costs something. The game never tells you that you chose well.
  • A tree that grows as you answer Your path is drawn as a tree, with harder optional questions hanging on it as fruit.
  • A life report at the end Your traits, your contradictions, and the archetype you turned out to be.
  • Plays offline No account, no sign-up. Progress stays on your phone unless you back it up to Play Games.

Ads and purchases

Selfbound is free. It shows ads, and watching one is always your choice for something in return — the path itself is never behind an ad. There is one optional purchase: an extra pack of questions. It is bought once and never expires.

What is collected, and by whom, is set out in the privacy policy. The rules you agree to when you play are in the terms of use.
