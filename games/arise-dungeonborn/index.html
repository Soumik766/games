What it is

You enter the dungeon alone and you don't leave that way. Every enemy you beat leaves a dark silhouette behind. Tap ARISE and it stands back up with glowing eyes, ready to fight for you. A lone summoner slowly becomes a whole legion of warriors, beasts, mages and fallen bosses, and you decide who marches, who gets healed and who gets left behind.

  • Raise what you defeatEnemies, elites and bosses can all join your legion, and each one fights the way it did against you.
  • Every shadow mattersWounds stay until you heal them, and a shadow at zero health is gone for good.
  • Treasure on every floorOpen chests for souls, gems and boss-worthy weapons, plus some very silly ones. Some chests bite back.
  • Plays offline, built for any phoneOne thumb to move, no connection needed, and graphics options for older devices.

Ads and purchases

ARISE: Dungeonborn is free and supported by ads: an occasional full-screen ad between floors, and short videos you can choose to watch for a revive, healing, gems or double rewards. Optional purchases include Remove Ads, bundles, gems, souls, commanders and cosmetics. Chests can't be bought, and the way forward is never behind an ad or a purchase.

You can play as a guest with no sign-in, or use Google Play Games to back up your progress to your own Google account.

What is collected, and by whom, is set out in the privacy policy. The rules you agree to when you play are in the terms of use.
