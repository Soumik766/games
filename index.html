About the studio

Boundless Team is an independent developer publishing on Google Play. We build small games with a lot of writing in them, on the belief that a game can ask a real question and be trusted with the answer.

Our games run offline, need no account, and never sell your data. Where a game carries ads or an optional purchase, it says so plainly on its own page and in the privacy policy.
