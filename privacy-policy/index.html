Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains what our games collect, who else receives it, and what you can do about it. It applies to every game published by Boundless Team ("we", "us"), on Google Play and anywhere else we publish, including games released after this policy was written.

The short version: our games do not ask who you are. Progress is kept on your own device. Ads and purchases are handled by Google, who do collect information — that is what the rest of this page is about.

Who we are

Boundless Team is an independent game developer publishing on Google Play. You can reach us at bsoumik103@gmail.com.

Boundless Team is the name an independent developer publishes games under. It is not a registered company, and there is no other entity behind it. Email is the way to reach us, and it reaches a person.

What we collect

We do not ask you for personal information, and our games have no accounts of our own. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or the contents of your device.

What a game stores, it stores on your device: your progress, your settings, and anything you typed into the game yourself, such as a display name you chose. We cannot read any of it.

If a game offers a Google Play Games sign-in and you choose to use it, your saved game is stored in your own Google account so it survives changing phones. That save belongs to you, sits in your Google Drive app-data area, and we do not have access to it beyond the game reading and writing your own save on your device.

Device and technical information

We do not operate servers that collect device information. However, the third-party services listed below — chiefly Google — do receive technical information when a game shows an ad, processes a purchase, or reports a crash. Typically that includes:

  • a device advertising identifier;
  • device model, operating system version, language and country;
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address (usually to city or region level);
  • information about the ad shown and whether it was viewed or tapped.

This is handled by those services under their own privacy policies, linked below.

Advertising

Our games may show ads supplied by Google AdMob. Ads are how a free game pays for itself. In our games, watching a rewarded ad is always something you choose to do in exchange for something in the game.

Google uses an advertising identifier and the technical information above to select and measure ads, and — with your consent where consent is required — to personalise them.

Personalised and non-personalised ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another region where this is required, the game asks for your consent the first time it runs, using Google's own consent form. You may choose non-personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings screen, where a "Privacy options" entry appears.

Non-personalised ads still use limited information, such as approximate location and general content context, to serve and measure the ad. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier in your Android settings, under Privacy or Ads.

Google's practices are described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Some of our games offer optional one-time purchases, sold through Google Play Billing. All payments are processed by Google. We never see or store your card number, billing address, or any other payment detail.

What the game learns is simply whether a given purchase is owned by the Google account signed in on the device, so it can unlock what was bought. That entitlement is stored on your device and confirmed with Google Play; it is not tied to any identity we hold.

Refunds and payment records are handled by Google Play under the Google Payments terms.

Analytics and crash reporting

Where a game uses analytics or crash reporting, it is used to understand how the game is played in aggregate and to find bugs — never to identify you. Crash reports typically contain the device model, the operating system version, and a technical stack trace of what the software was doing when it failed.

Not every game we publish uses these. Where a game does, the service is named on that game's page on this site.

Children's privacy

Our games are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Each game carries an age rating on its store listing; please respect it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, write to us and we will delete anything we hold.

Data retention

We do not run servers holding your data, so there is nothing for us to retain. Game progress stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the game. A Play Games save stays in your Google account until you delete it there. Third-party services retain what they collect according to their own policies.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstalling the game removes its local data, including progress and settings. Android Settings → Apps → the game → Storage → Clear data does the same without uninstalling.
  • Stop backing up: where a game offers Play Games backup, its Settings screen has a switch to turn it off. Nothing further is sent from that moment.
  • A Play Games save: delete it from your Google account at myaccount.google.com, under Play Games data, or from the Play Games app.
  • Signing out of Play Games: done in the Play Games app, under Settings. Current versions of Android do not let a game sign you out itself.
  • Your advertising identifier: reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Anything else: write to bsoumik103@gmail.com and we will help.

Security

Data stored on your device is protected by Android's own application sandbox, and any network traffic our games make uses encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but because we hold no personal information about you, there is very little about you to lose.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example under the GDPR in the EEA and the UK, or under the CCPA in California — you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export personal information held about you, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent you previously gave.

Since we hold no personal information ourselves, most such requests are answered by the steps in "Deleting your data" above, or need to be made to Google, who act as the controller for advertising and payment data. We will help you direct a request if you are not sure where it should go. We do not sell personal information.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our games change. The date at the top always shows the current version. If a change materially affects you, our games will ask you to read and accept the updated policy the next time you play.
